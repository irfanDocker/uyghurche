ℹ️ About Uyghurche

A free language preservation project for the Uyghur community and the world.

🌏 The Uyghur Language

Uyghur (ئۇيغۇر تىلى · Uyghur tili) is a beautiful Turkic language with a rich literary and cultural heritage spanning over a thousand years. It is closely related to Uzbek, Kazakh, and other Turkic languages of Central Asia. Uyghur is spoken by millions of people across Central Asia and in diaspora communities around the world.

✍️ The Script

Modern Uyghur uses a Perso-Arabic script adapted for Uyghur vowels, written right to left. It has 32 letters — 8 vowels and 24 consonants — and is one of the most phonetically complete Arabic-based scripts. A Latin romanization system (ULY — Uyghur Latin Yéziqi) is also used for learning and digital communication.

🎶 Culture & Heritage

The Uyghur people have a vibrant culture — famous for their music (the rawap, dutar, and tambur), poetry, cuisine (polo, samsa, lagman), dance, and carpet weaving. Classical Uyghur literature (the Twelve Muqam) is recognized as a UNESCO Intangible Cultural Heritage. Learning this language is a gateway to one of the world's oldest and most beautiful civilizations.

💡 Learning Tips

  • Start with the alphabet — use the 🔤 Alphabet page, click each card to hear it
  • Uyghur vowels are rich — ö, ü, é are different sounds, practice each carefully
  • Word order is Subject–Object–Verb, e.g. "Men alma yédim" = "I ate an apple"
  • Many words share roots with Turkish and Uzbek — use that connection
  • Use the 📚 Vocabulary Builder daily — even 5 minutes builds strong memory
  • Say words out loud — Uyghur has a flowing, musical sound
  • Take the 🎯 Level Test in the Games section to track your progress
