ℹ️ About Uyghurche

A free language preservation project for the Uyghur community and the world.

🌏 The Uyghur Language

Uyghur (ئۇيغۇر تىلى · Uyghur tili) is a Turkic language spoken by approximately 10–15 million people, primarily in the Xinjiang region of China and diaspora communities worldwide. It is closely related to Uzbek and other Central Asian languages.

✍️ The Script

Modern Uyghur uses a Perso-Arabic script adapted for Uyghur vowels, written right to left. It has 32 letters representing 8 vowels and 24 consonants. A Latin-based romanization system (ULY) and a Cyrillic version also exist.

ئا ئە ب پ ت ج چ خ د ر ز ژ س ش غ ف ق ك گ ڭ ل م ن ھ و ۇ ۆ ۈ ۋ ې ى ي

💡 Learning Tips

  • Practice the alphabet first — recognizing letters is the foundation
  • Uyghur vowels are rich — pay attention to ö, ü, é, and the distinction between o and u
  • Word order is Subject-Object-Verb (like Japanese/Korean), e.g. "Men alma yédim" = "I apple ate"
  • Many Uyghur words share roots with Turkish and Uzbek
  • Use flashcards daily — even 5 minutes builds strong memory
  • Try speaking out loud — Uyghur has a beautiful, flowing sound
